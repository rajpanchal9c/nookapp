Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27

The short version: Nook stores everything in your browser's localStorage. Nothing leaves your device unless you use the "Reflect with Nook" AI feature, in which case that day's journal text is sent to Groq (a third-party AI API provider) to generate a reflection. Nothing else you do in the app — journaling, tasks, moods, exports — is ever transmitted anywhere.

What we store

All journal entries, to-do items, moods, and app preferences (like sidebar state) are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. We do not operate a database, and we cannot see, access, or recover this data — if you clear your browser data or switch devices without exporting first, it's gone.

What "Reflect with Nook" sends, and to whom

When you tap "Reflect with Nook," the text of that day's journal entry (and your selected mood, if any) is sent to our serverless backend, which forwards it to Groq's API to generate a short reflection and extract possible tasks. This request is:

  • Sent only when you explicitly click the button — never automatically.
  • Not stored by Nook's backend. We don't log or retain journal content server-side.
  • Subject to Groq's own data handling — see Groq's privacy policy for how they process API requests.

Analytics

We use Umami, a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics tool, self-hosted by us, to see aggregate usage (page views, not journal content). It does not track you individually or across other sites.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no login, no personal identifiers collected.
  • No ads, no ad trackers, no selling of data (there's no data to sell).
  • No access to your journal content unless you trigger the AI reflection feature.

Your data, your control

Use Settings → Export Data to download a full JSON backup at any time, and Import Data to restore it on another device or browser. This is currently the only way to move your data between devices — Nook does not sync automatically.

Questions

Reach out via the feedback form linked in the app.
